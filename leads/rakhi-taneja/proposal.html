How we work each week

Welcome, anticipate, remember, look after.

Every customer payment is collected into DevShop's account first. Each week runs Monday to Sunday and is settled the following Monday by 1 PM, with an itemised statement — revenue, costs, marketing, fees and your profit split — and your share paid out. The backend is open to you the whole time. When something goes wrong, I own it, fix it, and leave you better off than before.

The terms

Standard DevShop terms, the same for every brand.

My share40% of gross profit. You keep 60%. Defined below so there's no ambiguity.
What "gross profit" meansThe money left from what customers pay after the direct cost of making and selling each order: product cost, packaging, shipping, payment-gateway fees, marketing/ad spend (always at cost), tech & admin subscriptions, and any Pay with a Post fee. Whatever remains is the gross profit we share — 40% to me, 60% to you. Every line is itemised on your Monday statement.
Deposit₹5,000, one time, by UPI — and fully adjustable against your actual onboarding tech costs (hosting, database, WhatsApp number, email, domain, AI). It goes toward your build; DevShop keeps none of it.
Term12 months, with a 30-day break clause. Renewable after the first year on mutually agreed terms.
Break clauseEither side can end with 30 days' notice. If it ends before 12 months, DevShop receives 5% of revenue generated through the store from the start date to the end date.
Your ad spendAlways yours, at cost. Everything else is covered in the stack.
Your brand & IPStays entirely yours, start to finish.

These are DevShop's published terms — the same for every brand. Full partnership terms and the mutual non-disclosure agreement are shared as documents before signing.
