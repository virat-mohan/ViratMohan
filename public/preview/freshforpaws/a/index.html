Questions

Is it really ready to eat?

Yes. No scooping, no defrosting, no guesswork: open the pack and serve.

What pack sizes are available?

Standard sizes are 100 g and 300 g for meals, 80 g for Liv-Love toppers. Multi-packs of 3, 6, 12 and 24 are available.

How much should my dog eat?

Use the plan builder: answer three questions and get the right daily portion for your dog's calorie needs.

Where do you deliver?

Delhi NCR, Gurugram and Noida today. Also available on Amazon, Flipkart, Supertails, Zigly and in pet stores.

Who makes the food?

Srishti Bhatia, a certified canine & feline nutritionist, who created every recipe herself after almost two years of research. She started Fresh For Paws on 13 June 2018, inspired by her dog Vanilla.
