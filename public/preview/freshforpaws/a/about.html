Our story

Started for Vanilla. Made for yours.

On 13 June 2018, Srishti Bhatia, a commerce graduate from Delhi University, started Fresh For Paws — inspired by her dog Vanilla.

She spent almost two years researching every ingredient and its nutritional value before the first meal. Today she is a certified canine & feline nutritionist, and every recipe is still hers.

What makes us different

100% natural

Real proteins, vegetables and fruit. No fillers. No synthetic vitamins or minerals added.

Ready to eat

No scooping, no defrosting, no guesswork. Open and serve. Convenient, light and easy to carry.

Pre-portioned

Every meal is portioned for your pet's calorie intake, based on nutritionist recommendations.
