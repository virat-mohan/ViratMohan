Questions

About the food

What is Fresh For Paws?

100% natural, ready-to-eat meals for dogs and cats. Every recipe is created by Srishti Bhatia, a certified canine & feline nutritionist, using real proteins, vegetables and fruit. No fillers, no synthetic vitamins or minerals.

Is it really ready to eat?

Yes. No scooping, no defrosting, no guesswork. Open the pack and serve at room temperature.

What recipes do you have?

Dogs (non-veg): Eggstravanza, Chicken Pot Pie, Fish Supper, Lamb On The Go, Wholesome Goat Feast.
Dogs (veg): Pumpkin It Up, A Green Affair, Oh My Greens, Go Go Cottage Cheese.
Cats: Purrfect Chicken Delight, Fin-tastic Fish Feast, Lamb Medley, Goat Goodness.
Puppies: Chick-a-Doodle.
Toppers: Liv-Love (Liver & Carrot, Liver & Pumpkin), Liverlicious, Peanut Butter Treat.

What pack sizes are available?

Meals come in 100 g and 300 g packs, with multi-packs of 3, 6, 12 and 24. Toppers are 80 g. Mini Paws (puppy) is 300 g.

Feeding

How much should my dog eat?

Use the plan builder: three questions and you get the right daily portion for your dog's calorie needs.

Can I mix it with kibble?

Yes. Many pet parents use Fresh For Paws as a topper or mix it in. For a full switch from kibble, transition over 7 to 10 days.

Is it suitable for puppies?

Mini Paws Chick-a-Doodle is made for puppies. It is complete & balanced with lean protein from chicken & lamb liver, rice, vegetables and curcumin.

Ordering & delivery

Where do you deliver?

Delhi NCR, Gurugram and Noida from the website. Also on Amazon, Flipkart, Supertails, Zigly, JustDogs, and in pet stores across India.

How do I order?

Shop on freshforpaws.com or use the plan builder for a personalised recommendation. Payment is prepaid (UPI / card).

About us

Who started Fresh For Paws?

Srishti Bhatia, a certified canine & feline nutritionist and commerce graduate from Delhi University, started Fresh For Paws on 13 June 2018, inspired by her dog Vanilla.

Any awards?

Pet Food of the Year, Indian Pet Industry Awards. Srishti: 2nd Runner Up, Pet Entrepreneur of the Year (Female), April 2021.
