What you actually get

Migration New

No re-listing every SKU by hand, no starting your reviews from zero. Your front end gets a brand new look too, built in your own brand design system — not a reused theme.

AI Content New

One photo in, a full shoot out

Take one picture of the product on your kitchen table. I generate the on-model shots, the lifestyle scenes, even short video — styled and ad-ready. This is exactly how Moonglasses gets new creative every week without booking a studio or a model.

Checkout

Pay with a Post™

A buyer posts about your product and unlocks a perk at checkout. It's a built-in acquisition loop — every order has a shot at becoming someone else's discovery post. Exclusive to DevShop Retail OS brands.

WhatsApp

Catalog to checkout, one tap

A product in a WhatsApp catalog message opens straight into a pre-filled cart on your real checkout — plus order updates, OTPs, and a shared inbox so you're not juggling a phone and a laptop to answer customers.

Recovery

The cart isn't lost, it's just idle

The moment someone starts typing at checkout and vanishes, I've already got their name, number and cart. A WhatsApp-first, two-stage sequence — a nudge, then a coupon — brings a real share of them back.

Growth Engine

Ads and content that draft themselves

When a product starts selling fast — or starts cooling off — the system drafts the ad brief, builds the campaign, and queues Instagram posts, paused and waiting for your yes. A recommendation engine running underneath surfaces the strategy to try next, pulled from your own sales data — you approve the spend; nothing launches on its own.
