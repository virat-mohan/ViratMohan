Questions

Which colour should I choose?

4300K is for all weather: warm light for rain and fog. 6000K is for a bright white driving style.

Will it blind oncoming drivers?

No. KORBI's precision-engineered beam pattern lights your path without blinding oncoming traffic.

What's in the box?

2 LED bulbs attached to the ballast, a fitting tool, zip ties and some cool stickers.

Can I fit it myself?

Yes. It's plug and play for your holder type, and the fitting tool is in the box.
