Questions

Good to know.

Why 8400 lumens and not "more watts"?

Wattage is power used, not light. Lux is intensity at one point. Lumens are the total light a bulb gives, and KORBI gives 8,400 from 70W.

How does the warranty work?

One year from purchase, plus a second year when you register the product online. Claims: call +91 96677 76763 or write to help@korbi.in with your proof of purchase.

Who should fit them?

A certified, professional technician. Fitting by anyone else voids the warranty and replacement rights.

What if a bulb doesn't work out of the box?

Send a video showing the fault and no damage within 7 working days of delivery, and KORBI replaces it with a new unit. Refunds are only for confirmed manufacturing defects.

When will it arrive?

Orders leave in 1–2 business days and reach you in 3–11 working days, anywhere in India. You get a tracking number on dispatch.
