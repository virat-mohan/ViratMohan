Moonglasses makes light-tint eyewear. Light tints, good vibe. Anun Dhawan and I own it together: he runs the range and the suppliers, and DevShop Retail OS runs the rest.

### What went live

- **The store,** with UPI payments checked on the server, so the price a customer pays is always the price on the page.
- **Free shipping** built into the price, and one clear discount per order.
- **Stock** taken only when a payment is confirmed, all or nothing, so the last pair is never sold twice.
- **Pay With A Post™.** A customer posts their pair with their own code. When two friends order with it, their pair ships free, and every two more earns another pair.
- **One brand book** behind every word the store, its emails and its posts say.

### What happens now

The store is run for the brand: orders, payments, shipping, customer care and content, with results every Monday.

If you have a product people love, I'll build the machine that gets it to the world.
