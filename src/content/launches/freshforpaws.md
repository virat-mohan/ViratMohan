Fresh For Paws is Srishti Bhatia's brand. She's a certified canine nutritionist who has made ready-to-eat natural meals for dogs, cats and puppies since 2018.

India's pet population grew from 28.4 million to 39.2 million between 2020 and 2025, yet about 9 in 10 bags of pet food sold are still dry kibble (IMARC, 2025).

DevShop Retail OS now runs the machine behind it: orders, payments, delivery, marketing and reporting.
