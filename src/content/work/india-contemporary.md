If your business is two-sided — buyers and sellers, collectors and makers — this same marketplace structure can be licensed and built for you: the catalogue, the no-login application flow, the admin tools. A fixed monthly licence or revenue-share, scoped on a call.

Indian contemporary artists have almost no direct route into European collector demand. This platform is built to be that route — a two-sided marketplace with a full curation layer behind it.

### Discovery & Buying

- A browsable catalogue with true-proportion artwork images, a sold-works archive as proof of real transactions, and an enquiry form built directly onto each piece.

### Joining, Made Effortless For Artists

- A full application — details, artwork, agreement, e-signature — completed **without ever creating a password**.
- Contracts generated and emailed automatically the moment an application is approved.

### Running the Gallery

- An internal dashboard for the full roster, with duplicate detection, staged publishing, and cascading cleanup when an artist is removed.

The part invisible from browsing is what makes "apply without an account" actually work: a secure hand-off lets an artist upload photos before any login exists, while every table stays locked down so only published work is ever visible to the public. It carries four represented artists today, with a running sold-works archive already on record.
