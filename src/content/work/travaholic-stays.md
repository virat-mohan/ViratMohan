A boutique villa business does not need to run on borrowed infrastructure — Airbnb for discovery, WhatsApp for negotiation, a spreadsheet for who owes what. This system replaces all three with one branded platform: a real booking engine, a single place for pricing and availability, and automation for every piece of paperwork that used to be typed out by hand. It runs a live portfolio of twelve villas across Goa, Mussoorie, and Himachal Pradesh today, on a real payment gateway, not a demo.

### The Booking Experience

- A **villa catalogue** guests can filter by location, group size, bedrooms, pool, and price — full photo galleries, video, house rules, and a map location that fills in from a single pasted link.
- **Pricing that updates itself** as a guest picks dates: weekday and weekend rates, special pricing for peak nights, discounts for longer stays, and every fee included, so the number a guest sees is the number they pay.
- A guest can book directly — pick dates, add extras like a private chef, apply a coupon, and see the full price before submitting.
- Every enquiry, whether it is a booking request, a callback, or a property owner asking to list, lands sorted by what it is actually about — nothing sits in one undifferentiated inbox.
- A content and search layer built in, so the site itself brings guests in, not just handles them once they arrive.

### Running the Portfolio

- Every listing — photos, pricing, availability — editable in one place, with a one-click switch to keep a property private for negotiated deals without it ever appearing on the public site.
- **Property owners get their own private view** — invited once, they set their own password, and from then on see only their own properties, their calendar, and exactly what they have earned, booking by booking.
- A private internal dashboard for the team running the business, separate from anything a guest or owner ever sees.

### The Calendar That Never Lies

- A booking's dates only lock once a **payment is actually made** — so an unpaid enquiry can never accidentally block out a paying guest, and if a paid booking's dates change, the calendar updates itself automatically.
- One calendar view per villa, colour-coded by where the booking came from, plus a single list showing every booking across every property at once.
- **Two-way sync with Airbnb** — book a villa here, and it blocks on Airbnb automatically; get booked on Airbnb, and it blocks here — with a one-click link straight back to that reservation on Airbnb's own dashboard, so nothing needs to be checked twice.

### Payments, Without the Manual Math

- Card and UPI payments built in, with a guided setup so connecting a payment account takes minutes, not a developer.
- Anything paid offline — cash, bank transfer — can be logged manually and still triggers the same receipt and confirmation a guest would get from an online payment.
- **Owner payouts calculate themselves** — every villa has its own commission rate, and what the property owner is owed is worked out automatically, booking by booking, with the option to adjust it for a one-off deal.
- Full financial reporting, by property and by owner, ready to export.
- A separate path for hand-negotiated deals — a custom rate, a security deposit, extras — that generates a real payment link and a proper document to send a guest directly.

### Every Guest Gets a Real Document

- Every booking proposal and confirmation is a branded, professional document generated automatically — not a template someone fills in by hand.
- Guests are kept updated by email and WhatsApp at every stage — the booking proposal, payment received, confirmation — automatically, without anyone typing a message.

The part that actually mattered here was not the booking form. It was making sure a villa's calendar never lies — a date only locks once money is real, and every change stays in sync automatically, in both directions, even against a system as locked-down as Airbnb's. That is the difference between a booking page and something that actually runs a rental business.
