This same system can be licensed for your own rental or booking business — a fixed monthly licence, or revenue-share instead, scoped to what you need. New features worked out on a call.

Travaholic Stays replaces Airbnb, WhatsApp, and a spreadsheet with one branded platform, running a live portfolio of twelve villas across Goa, Mussoorie, and Himachal Pradesh on a real payment gateway.

### Booking & Pricing

- A filterable villa catalogue with pricing that updates itself — weekday/weekend rates, peak pricing, long-stay discounts, all live as a guest picks dates.
- Guests book directly: dates, add-ons, coupon, full price shown before submitting.

### A Calendar That Never Lies

- Dates only lock once **payment is actually made** — an unpaid enquiry can never block a paying guest.
- **Two-way Airbnb sync** — book here or on Airbnb, and both calendars update automatically.

### Owners & Payouts

- Property owners get their own private view — their calendar, their earnings, booking by booking.
- **Owner payouts calculate themselves** from each villa's commission rate, with financial reporting ready to export.

### Every Guest Gets a Real Document

- Branded booking proposals and confirmations, generated automatically and sent by email and WhatsApp at every stage.

The part that actually mattered was making sure the calendar never lies — a date locks only when money is real, and stays in sync in both directions, even against a system as locked-down as Airbnb's.
