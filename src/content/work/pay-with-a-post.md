Moon Glasses is a D2C eyewear brand. Its checkout does something no ordinary D2C store does: a customer can pay with a post instead of money — no payment gateway involved at all for that flow.

### How it actually works

- Over 5,000 followers, verified live via Instagram Business Discovery (never self-reported): the order ships immediately, on trust — but only after the shopper proves they control the handle by placing a one-time code in their bio for a few minutes, and agrees to post within 12 hours of delivery.
- Under that bar, or once the day's trust-ship slots are full: nothing ships yet. The shopper gets a unique, shareable code instead, and their own order ships the moment that code drives three real, paid orders from other people.
- The code is a genuine coupon under the hood — full price for whoever redeems it. This is attribution, not a discount mechanic.

It's a real barter economy built into checkout: distribution earns inventory, verified server-side at every step, with the same fulfilment path as a normal paid order once it qualifies.
