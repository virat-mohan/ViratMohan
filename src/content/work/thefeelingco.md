If your business runs on enquiries — weddings, events, any boutique service business — this same system can be built for you. A custom-scoped engagement; the fastest way to a number is a short call.

Before this, every enquiry came in by email and either got answered or got buried, with no consistent way to price a proposal and no real presence on Google. What was built instead is a full digital operation: brand site, lead capture, internal tools, and content.

### A Brand Site That Converts

- A full-screen opening film, a founder story page, and a bespoke brand system carried across all eleven pages.
- An 8-step enquiry form that never overwhelms, plus one-tap WhatsApp.

### Nothing Gets Missed

- Every enquiry is captured through two independent paths at once — a lead can never quietly vanish.
- A private, searchable dashboard of every enquiry, replacing hours of digging through an inbox.

### Proposals, Done Properly

- A private tool turns what used to be a typed WhatsApp message into a real document — pricing calculator, live budget tracker, and a one-click branded PDF export.

### Built To Be Found

- Full technical SEO and structured data on every page, plus a seven-piece content library, each written around a real search query and ending in a path back to the enquiry form.

The technical build shipped as nine separate, reviewed releases — proof this runs at the pace a real business needs, not an agency's idea of a timeline.
