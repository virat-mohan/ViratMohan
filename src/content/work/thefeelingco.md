Before this, every enquiry came in by email and either got answered or got buried. There was no way to search past enquiries without digging through an inbox, no consistent way to price a client proposal, and searching the business's own name on Google surfaced a stale placeholder page instead of the actual brand. What was built instead is a complete digital operation, not just a website: a brand site, lead capture, internal tools to run the business day to day, and a content engine — all running without a single external subscription.

### A Brand Site That Actually Converts

- A full-screen opening film that welcomes every visitor properly — and, unlike most sites like this, plays correctly every single time someone returns, including using the browser's own back button.
- A founder story page — the person, the photography, the narrative behind the brand — with a matching printable version.
- A brochure that prints perfectly from any browser, generated automatically from the same brand materials, with zero manual layout work.
- An enquiry form that never overwhelms — one short question at a time instead of one long intimidating page, walking a couple through celebration type, date, guest count, priorities, and budget naturally.
- One-tap WhatsApp from any page, message already written.
- A complete bespoke look — colours, type, consistent components, a gentle signature animation — carried identically across all eleven pages, on every device.

### Nothing Gets Missed

- Every enquiry is captured through two independent paths at once, so if one ever has a hiccup, the other still gets it through — a lead can never quietly vanish.
- A private, searchable view of every enquiry ever received, click-to-call and click-to-email built in — replacing hours of digging through an inbox.
- Automatic filtering of spam and bot submissions before they ever reach the business.

### Proposals, Done Properly

An internal tool, kept private and separate from the public site, turns what used to be a manually typed WhatsApp message into a real, professional document:

- A pricing calculator across every core service the business offers.
- A live budget tracker that flags automatically the moment a proposal goes over what a client said they wanted to spend.
- A payment schedule that splits automatically across the stages of an event, checks its own math, and shows real numbers, not estimates.
- Saved, reusable proposals, and a one-click export into a polished, print-ready document — no typing the same terms out twice.

### Built To Be Found

- Full search-engine groundwork on every page, so the site is properly readable by Google and by AI search tools, not just by people.
- A seven-piece content library — a planning checklist, a local pricing guide, planning guides by event type, a destination-versus-local guide, a vendor-timing guide, a wedding-season guide — each one written around a real question a couple actually searches for, and every single one ending in a direct path back to the enquiry form, never a dead end.

### Built To Last

- No moving parts to break — every page loads near-instantly, with nothing to install, update, or maintain.
- No server sitting idle, running up a bill, when nobody's visiting.
- Every change is reviewed before it goes live, with a full record of what changed and when.

The parts that took real engineering are, correctly, invisible. An enquiry is captured through two systems at once so a single outage never costs a lead. The tool that keeps the business logged into its own systems kept working, untouched, straight through a provider changing how logins work behind the scenes — the kind of change that quietly breaks most simple integrations. And the proposal tool computes real numbers live, in front of the person using it, not as a static form pretending to be a calculator. The technical build alone shipped as nine separate, reviewed releases — the search-engine foundation, then seven content pieces across two rounds — proof this runs at the pace a real business needs, not an agency's idea of a timeline.
