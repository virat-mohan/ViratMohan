Moonglasses runs on one stack from storefront to spend: catalog, checkout, shipping, WhatsApp, ads, and an AI content engine that replaces the studio shoot most small eyewear brands can't afford.

### Storefront & Photography

- A homepage "trending now" strip that refreshes daily from real traffic, not a manual edit.
- **One photo of the product in, on-model shots and short video out** — no studio booking, no model day rate.
- A UGC wall of real customer photos, submitted, moderated, and auto-tagged to the product.

### Checkout & Recovery

- Guest checkout, live pincode-based shipping rates, prepaid or cash-on-delivery with a small advance.
- A two-stage, **WhatsApp-first abandoned-cart sequence** — a plain nudge, then a coupon — catches carts the moment someone starts typing and vanishes.

### WhatsApp & Meta

- A WhatsApp catalog message opens straight into a pre-filled cart on the real checkout — plus order updates, OTPs, and a shared inbox to reply from.
- Meta pixel with a server-side mirror so a sale is counted once, not twice; ad briefs draft themselves off real sales signals, paused for a human yes before any spend goes out.

### Numbers, Honestly

- Day-by-day ad spend versus revenue, a real monthly profit & loss from actual orders and refunds, and a daily digest — always current, never a spreadsheet someone forgot to update.

The part that doesn't show up in a demo is the one that matters most for a brand this size: nobody had to hire a photographer, a media buyer, or a second person to answer WhatsApp. The system does the volume; a founder still decides what ships.
