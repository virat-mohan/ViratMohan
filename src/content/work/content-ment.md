Content teams typically stitch together a drafting tool, a separate approval chain (usually email or Slack), and a third tool for export and scheduling — with the actual status of any piece of content living in someone's head. Content-ment puts all three stages inside one workflow, so nothing is waiting on a thread nobody can find.

### The workflow, not just the drafts

- AI-assisted drafting that starts from the brand's actual voice and facts, not a blank page.
- A built-in approval stage — one place to see what's pending, what's approved, and what's blocked, instead of a parallel conversation in another app.
- Export straight from the approved draft, so the last-touched version is always the one that ships.
