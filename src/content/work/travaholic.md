Travaholic needed to run like a real business from day one — not six subscriptions stitched together with someone's evenings and weekends. So the entire commerce backbone was built from scratch: the storefront, the order desk, the shipping desk, the marketing engine, and the finance desk, wired together and talking to each other from the start. What most small e-commerce teams spend months assembling — and thousands a year renting as separate tools — went live in two days.

### Store & Products

- **Product pages** with a 360°/multi-angle photo viewer, not a static image.
- Collections and homepage, fully editable with no code.
- Discount codes and automatic sale rules.
- **"Notify me"** capture on sold-out items, so a stockout still converts eventually.
- Reviews, collected automatically after delivery — no manual follow-up needed.

### Checkout & Payments

- Card, UPI, and netbanking checkout, built in.
- Cash-on-delivery with a partial advance payment, not all-or-nothing.
- Guest checkout, or a real account with order history.
- Gifting, with a gift note.
- Automatic, downloadable invoices for every order.

### Shipping & Returns

- Courier booking, labels, and live tracking — automatic, not a daily manual task.
- Tracking stays current even if a courier update is missed.
- A failed delivery **nudges the customer automatically** instead of quietly becoming a lost sale.
- A returned parcel triggers refund and restock **by itself**.
- A simple return/exchange flow for customers, with no back-and-forth emails.

### Bringing Customers Back

- A loyalty points system, on rules the business controls.
- A referral program with automatic rewards.
- Abandoned-cart reminders by email and WhatsApp.
- A "we miss you" flow that re-engages quiet customers on its own.
- Best customers, and at-risk ones, flagged automatically — not buried in a spreadsheet.

### Marketing, On Autopilot

- AI writes the ad copy and generates the ad image.
- One click launches it on Meta — paused, so spend is always approved by a human first.
- A content calendar that posts and launches on schedule.
- A blog and a newsletter, drafted automatically.
- A WhatsApp inbox that can be read and answered without touching a phone.

### Knowing the Numbers

- Visitors, sales, and drop-off, in plain charts — not a raw analytics dashboard nobody reads.
- See exactly which ads and channels actually sell, not just which ones spend.
- Profit & loss and expenses, always current.
- A daily summary — new orders, refunds, low stock — without opening five tabs.
- An assistant that trims underperforming ad spend automatically.

None of this is templated storefront theming. The parts that actually took engineering are the ones a customer never sees: a return that restocks itself, a courier update that stays accurate even when the courier's own system misses a beat, an ad-spend assistant that quietly cuts what isn't working. That is the difference between a website and an operating system for a business — and it is the same principle behind every product on this page: AI does the volume, a human still decides what matters.
