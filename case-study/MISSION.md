# Mission, vision, fundamentals

**Mission.** Give any product founder a working online business in 7 days, run for them, with results they can see every Monday, and prove it in public with real numbers.

**Vision.** A new, more efficient way for commerce to work: one operator plus machines running thousands of brands better than a full in-house team, paid only from the value it creates, and sharing that value with everyone who helps.

## Fundamentals (every decision is checked against these)

1. **Fair practice.** Standard terms, clearly published. No hidden fees. Ad spend at cost. Nobody is locked in beyond what they signed.
2. **Transparency.** Founders see their numbers every day and are settled every Monday. Partners see every rupee on their dashboard. Every public claim has a source, and small bases are said out loud.
3. **Collective growth.** Brands keep their IP. Customers who post get rewarded (Pay with a Post). Partners earn 25% for as long as their brand stays. Staff are paid for results, not hours.
4. **Positive economic impact.** Small makers get a real brand and keep more margin than marketplaces allow. Local jobs in packing, shipping, content and ops. Formal, GST-registered businesses.
5. **Efficiency.** Machines do the routine; people only where trust, money or judgement need them. Live in 7 days today, faster every month, profitable from day 1.
6. **Plain language.** Anyone, anywhere should understand what we do and what it costs in one read.
7. **Keep promises.** Never compromise "live in 7 days", on-time shipping, or honest numbers to look good.
