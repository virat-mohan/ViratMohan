# The reel launch SOP: the first hour earns the reach

Every brand on DevShop Retail OS™ launches a reel the same way. The reach a reel gets is decided in its first hour, mostly by signals I can send myself — not by luck and not by ad spend. This is the operational layer under the growth machine (`GROWTH-MACHINE.md`, channels 1 and 2): the reel is the hook, and the first hour is how I give it the best chance to travel before I ever pay to boost it.

Why it matters: my job is to take something good a founder made and build the machine that gets it to the world (`/mission`). Organic reach is the cheapest order the brand will ever get. Working the first hour well is how I lower the overall cost per order without leaning on Meta.

**Retail OS classification:** core Retail OS capability under **Marketing** / **Acquisition**. It is configuration per brand (keyword, CTA, landing link, which handles reshare), not a bespoke build. Every brand gets the same SOP; the words change, the method doesn't.

Everything here is a legitimate first-party action — things I'd do from the brand's own real accounts. No bought likes, no bot comments, no fake or throwaway accounts, no engagement pods. Those break Instagram's terms and they'd fail our own values (`/mission`: the responsible way). If a step can't be done honestly from a real account the brand owns, it doesn't happen.

## Before the reel goes out (set once, per brand, approved)

Nothing below gets posted without Virat's approval. These are drafted and shown to him first; the keyword, question, CTA and landing link are brand config, confirmed with the founder:

- **The keyword** — one short, on-brand word tied to the product (e.g. `CAP`, `LINK`, the product name). Same keyword in the caption, the pinned comment and the comment-to-DM automation, so it all points one way.
- **The CTA line** — the lead magnet: "Comment **`<keyword>`** and I'll send you the link / the full breakdown." This is the one job the caption has to do.
- **The question** — one genuine question for the audience that invites a reply, not a yes/no.
- **The landing link** — the reel-matched landing or product page, with the right UTM (`utm_source=reel&utm_campaign=<reel>`, per the growth machine). Fetch it and confirm it loads (200) before the reel goes live — never link a page before it's deployed.
- **Voice check** — the caption, the three self-comments and the reply templates all pass the brand's `checkVoice()` (`BRAND-BOOK-STANDARD.md`) before approval.

## The first 60 minutes (the checklist)

Run top to bottom the moment the reel is live. All of it is done from accounts the brand genuinely owns.

1. **Like and save the reel** from the brand account, straight away. A first-party signal that the post is worth engaging with, before the feed decides who sees it.
2. **Share it to the brand's story** immediately, with a tap-through sticker or the link. Keeps the reel in front of the people already following, and sends them back to it.
3. **Post the three self-comments, in this order, and pin the first one:**
   - a) **The keyword comment** — just the keyword and the CTA ("Comment `<keyword>` for the link"). **Pin it to the top immediately** so it's the first thing anyone reads.
   - b) **The question** — the audience question, to open conversations in the thread.
   - c) **The CTA** — the full call to action and the offer.
4. **Reshare from the brand's other real handles** — the founder's handle and @viratmohan_devshop where the rules allow it (`FOUNDER-SOCIAL-RULES.md`: partner brands only, milestone posts only, Virat's email approval, disclose the connection). Like, save and reshare to story from each. Only genuine accounts the brand or founder actually owns — never a handle created to inflate numbers.
5. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Real replies that ask something back and keep the thread going, never one word. A live thread tells the feed the reel is worth showing to more people, and it's also just good hospitality (`PLAYBOOK.md`).
6. **Pin every comment that uses the keyword.** As real viewers use it, pin theirs too. Repeated keyword use in a reel's comments is a searchable signal — it helps the reel show up when someone searches that term.
7. **Fulfil the lead magnet.** Every keyword comment triggers the comment-to-DM flow (growth machine, channel 1): the auto-reply plus a DM with the link, inside Meta's 24-hour window. Confirm the automation fired for the first few by hand.

## The keyword method, in one line

One keyword, everywhere, consistently: in the caption, pinned by me on my own comment, and pinned again on every real viewer who uses it. That repetition is what makes the reel findable in search later — comment SEO, done with real comments, not planted ones.

## The CTA mechanic: the keyword lead magnet

"Comment `<keyword>` and I'll send it" does three jobs at once: it drives comments (reach), it opens a DM thread I'm allowed to message in for 24 hours (the sale), and it tells me exactly who's interested (a warm list). The DM carries the reel-matched link so the order is tracked back to the reel. The reward has to be real — the link, the guide, whatever I promised — delivered every time.

## Who decides what

- **Virat approves** every reel, caption, keyword, CTA and reply template before anything goes out. Nothing is posted, DM'd or automated without his approval. Accepting any collab invite stays manual in the app.
- **The founder confirms** brand facts behind the reel — product, price, stock, offer. A gap in the brand book is asked, never guessed (`BRAND-BOOK-STANDARD.md`).
- **Config per brand:** the keyword, the CTA line, the landing link, and which handles reshare. Set once, reused every launch.
- **I measure, not guess:** orders per reel show on the founder console ("Where orders came from"). The claims behind each step — that shares and early engagement help reach, that comment keywords help search — are the reasoning for doing them; the only numbers I ever report are the brand's real results from the console and the ad account. ## What I never do

No bought engagement, no bots, no fake or burner accounts, no engagement pods, no asking people to comment in exchange for anything that isn't the honest lead magnet. Every signal in this SOP is a real action from a real account. If it can't be done that way, I don't do it — and I flag it to Virat instead.
