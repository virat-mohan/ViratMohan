## 3. Name, tagline, positioning
- Running copy: "Moonglasses". Logo wordmark: "MOON GLASSES™". Page titles: "Moonglasses™".
- Tagline: "Light tints, good vibe".
- One-line vibe: a late-night listening room that happens to sell glasses.

- North-star test: does this feel like a place people drift into, or a shop asking for something? If it asks, rewrite it.

## 4. Voice
- Attributes: casual not careless; cool not trying too hard; warm not overly friendly; confident never arrogant; selective not exclusive; fun not silly. Posture: unbothered.
- Do: talk about us, not you-the-customer; buttons are labels, not commands ("Press play", "See the drop", "Look around"); short lines and space; ritual over urgency; specific founder moments; small case except headlines; max two mood words per piece.
- Don't: wallet imperatives (shop, buy, grab yours, deal); fake urgency; "luxury", "premium", "affordable luxury"; "elevate", "must-have", "iconic", "game-changer"; "Dear customer", "You need this".
- Label swaps: Shop → "See the edit"; Add to Cart → "Keep this one"; Limited Time Offer → "Here for now"; Limited Series → "The limited edit"; The Collection → "The main edit".
- Banned: supplier (Ted Smith) model names in customer copy; "Ted Smith" anywhere except the credit line "Moonglasses × Ted Smith"; weekly-mix lines until the mix exists.
- Retired: "Gift First", "5,000 followers", "we ship first"; old taglines "Light tints, big vibe", "See A Brighter You", "Light Tints Big Mood".

## 5. Visual

- Fonts: Space Grotesk (primary), Inter (secondary), Bodoni Moda (editorial italic).
- Logo: MOON wordmark, ™ at upper-right of the N, GLASSES below in small italic widely tracked caps. Champagne gold on black, black on white. Never with a sunglasses icon or moon illustration.
- Photo: golden-hour and night-out flash, candid friend groups, faces half behind lenses, negative space, one bold frame per image. Metal frames silver or black only, never gold. If an image looks like it is selling, it is wrong.
