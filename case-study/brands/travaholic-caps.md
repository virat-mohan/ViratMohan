## 3. Name, tagline, positioning
- Name: "Travaholic" (brand), "Travaholic Caps" internally.
- Tagline: "Stories You Can Wear".
- Positioning: premium trucker caps made in India, each inspired by a real place or journey. Flat ₹1,399, ships across India.
- Terms: a cap design is a "Chapter"; collections are the "Story Series"; customers "Explorers" (site) / "Travaholics" (emails); campaign line "Postcards from India".

## 4. Voice
- Attributes: warm, specific, editorial travel storytelling; confident, never shouty; never a hard sell.
- Do: tie every cap to a real place or moment; name the place, the light, the moment; credit a Chapter like a magazine credits an outfit; when an offer is mentioned, lead with free shipping on prepaid orders, then Buy 3, Get 1 Free.
- Don't: hard sell; stacked exclamation marks; discount %, "sale" or coupon codes in ads; implying a markdown on ₹1,399; WELCOMEBACK15 outside email; BUYNOW10 outside the abandoned-cart nudge.
- Hard-sell words (warn): hurry, act now, last chance, don't miss out, grab yours now, buy now, limited stock, while stocks last.
- Retired: Cash on Delivery / COD (removed 26 Sep 2026, prepaid only).
