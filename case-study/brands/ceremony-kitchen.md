## 4. Voice
- Attributes: ritualistic, refined, sensory, warm, intentional.
- Do: fewer, well-chosen words; evoke texture, warmth, aroma; quiet luxury; warm like a thoughtful host, invite don't instruct; ritual, not routine. Health (gluten-free, sugar-free, keto) integrated, not shouted.
- Don't: loud or salesy ("Buy now!", "Limited time offer!"); over-explain; clinical ("high in fiber", "glycemic", calories, macros); trendy slang ("obsessed", "game changer", "vibes"); adjective stacks.
- Banned: "Truth and Taste", "Truth & Taste", "#TruthAndTaste", and the old name "In The Know Kitchen".
- Stale: order-by "1 Nov" (now 6 Nov).
