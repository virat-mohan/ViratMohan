What you asked for

From Raghu's note of 27 September, in your own numbers:

  • WhatsApp is your main channel, and it leaks. Under 20% of messages sent are delivered. 50% of those are seen, 20% of seen get a reply, under 10% of replies order. Under 1% of messages sent become an order.
  • No extra staff. You want it automated.
  • Trial orders from the earlier Iredus database.
  • Custom-label gifting for birthdays, anniversaries, corporates, planners, hotels and restaurants.
  • Referrals, your best channel, measured and built into a programme.
  • Later: younger buyers (25 to 35), bars and B2B, lifestyle partners, packaging.

How I'd approach it

Fix the pipe before pouring more in. Delivery is the first leak. When four in five messages never arrive, every later step is working on a fifth of the audience. So I'd move WhatsApp to the official Business API, let customers order and pay inside the chat, and only then run campaigns.

Marketing and branding (the younger-audience campaign, packaging, partnerships) come in phase two, once weekly orders are steady.

The plan

Four phases, sales first

Week 1 · the 7-day build
Official WhatsApp Business API number and Meta Business Verification, started on day 1 because it can take weeks. The store on aloochips.com with ordering and UPI payment from inside WhatsApp, so no one has to reply by hand. Order confirmations and shipping updates by WhatsApp and email. A "Who told you about us?" question at checkout.
Weeks 2–3 · the database
Clean and segment the earlier Iredus list. Only message people who agreed to hear from Iredus; everyone else gets a one-time ask to opt in. One trial-pack offer with a one-tap order. Send in small batches, measure each step, change one thing at a time.
Weeks 3–4 · gifting
A gifting page: custom label with a name and message, occasion date, delivery date. Birthdays and anniversaries saved, with a reminder before the date comes round again. A bulk and corporate quote form that comes to you to approve.
Month 2 · referrals
Each buyer gets their own code, with credit for the referrer and a thank-you for the friend, tracked in orders. Pay with a Post™ for customers who'd rather share than pay full price.
