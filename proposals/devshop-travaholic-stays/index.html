What we built

01

Booking & pricing

  • Filterable villa catalogue
  • Prices update themselves: weekday and weekend rates, peak pricing, long-stay discounts
  • Guests book direct with dates, add-ons and coupon, full price shown before they submit
02

A calendar that never lies

  • Dates lock only once payment is made, so an unpaid enquiry never blocks a paying guest
  • Two-way Airbnb sync: book on either and both calendars update
03

Owners & payouts

  • Each owner gets a private view of their calendar and earnings, booking by booking
  • Payouts calculate themselves from each villa's commission rate
  • Financial reports ready to export
04

Guest documents

  • Branded booking proposals and confirmations, generated automatically
  • Sent by email and WhatsApp at every stage
05

Your brand

  • One branded platform in place of listings spread across apps
06

Licensable

  • The same system can run your rental business, scoped to what you need
  • New features worked out on a call

What mattered mostMaking sure the calendar never lies. A date locks only when money is real, and stays in sync both ways, even against a system as locked-down as Airbnb's.
