The ground rule

Alcohol ads are banned in India (Cable TV Networks rules), surrogate ads are banned under the 2022 CCPA guidelines, and Meta does not allow alcohol ads in India. So no paid Meta/Google ads for Virasat in India. This plan is fully online: content, creators and search, and Radico's legal team clears every piece.
