The ground rule

Alcohol ads are banned in India (Cable TV Networks rules), surrogate ads are banned under the 2022 CCPA guidelines, and Meta does not allow alcohol ads in India. So no paid Meta/Google ads for Virasat in India. The plan below is built for that: organic, owned and retail-led, age-gated, with every piece cleared by Radico's legal team.
